MINI QPR

Mini Qadirpur Rawan — a free mini open-world game set in the town of Qadirpur Rawan, Pakistan. Drive cars and auto rickshaws along Khanewal Road, visit real landmarks like the Passport Office, Zaheer Hospital and Jamia Masjid Muhammadia Salfia, meet the morning milkman, and complete six missions.

No download needed — the game runs right in your browser, on phone or computer.
